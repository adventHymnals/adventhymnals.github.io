Hymns and Tunes 1886 162

Let thy Spirit, blessed Saviour

Studio

1Let thy Spirit, blessed Saviour,
Come and bid our doubtings cease;
Come, O, come with love and favor,
Fill us all with joy and peace.

2Fearful dangers are around us,
Satan watches to destroy;
Lord, our foes would fain confound us;
O, for thy might employ!

3On thy word and souls subsisting,
Taught by thee, thy name we love;
Sweet'st of all names is Jesus;
How it doth our spirits move!

4Let us not, O Lord, be weary
Of thy roughness of the way;
Though the road be often dreary,
Thou shalt drive our fears away.

Words
Anon
Music
D. E. Jones
Tune
Stockwell
Meter
