Hymns and Tunes 1886 351

Come, Thou Almighty King

Words: AnonymousMusic: Felice de GiardiniTune: Italian Hymn6.6.4.6.6.6.4

Studio

1Come, thou almighty King,
Help us thy name to sing,
Help us to praise:
Father all-glorious,
O'er all victorious,
Come, and reign over us,
Ancient of days.

2Come, thou incarnate Word,
Gird on thy mighty sword,
Our prayer attend:
Come, and thy people bless,
And give thy word success:
Spirit of holiness,
On us descend.
