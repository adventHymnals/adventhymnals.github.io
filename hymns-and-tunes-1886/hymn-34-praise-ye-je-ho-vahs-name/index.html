Hymns and Tunes 1886 34

Praise ye Je-ho-vah's name

Music: Henry CareyTune: America6s & 4s

Studio

1Praise ye Je-ho-vah's name, Praise through his courts proclaim,
Rise and a-dore. High o'er the
heavens a-bove, Sound his great acts of love,
While his rich grace we prove
Fast as his power.

2Now let the trumpet raise
Sounds of triumphant praise,
Wide as his fame.
There let the harp be found;
Organs of solemn sound,
Roll your deep notes around,
Filled with his name.

3While his high praise you sing,
Shake every sounding string;
Sweet the accord!
He vital breath bestows;
Let every breath that flows,
His noble fame disclose;
Praise ye the Lord.
