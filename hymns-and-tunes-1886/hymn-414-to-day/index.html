Hymns and Tunes 1886 414

To-day

Music: Lowell MasonTune: To-day6s & 4s

Studio

1To-day the Saviour calls;
Ye wanderers, come;
O ye benighted souls,
Why longer roam?

2To-day the Saviour calls!
O listen now;
Within these sacred walls,
To Jesus bow.

3To-day the Saviour calls!
For refuge fly;
The storm of vengeance falls,
And death is nigh.

4To-day the Saviour calls!
For refuge fly;
The storm of vengeance falls;
Ruin is nigh.

5The Spirit calls to-day!
Yield to his power;
O grieve it not away,
'Tis mercy's hour.
