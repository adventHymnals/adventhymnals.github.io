Hymns and Tunes 1886 183

How shall the young secure their hearts

Words: Isaac WattsMusic: George N. AllenTune: MAITLANDC. M.

Studio

1How shall the young secure their hearts
And guard their lives from sin?
Thy word the choicest rules imparts
To keep the conscience clean.

2'Tis like the sun, a heavenly light,
That guides me all the day;
And through the dangers of the night,
A lamp to lead the way.

3Thy precepts make me truly wise;
I hate the sinner's road;
I hate my own vain thoughts that rise,
But love thy law, my God.

4Thy word is everlasting truth;
How pure is every page!
That holy book shall guide my youth,
And well support my age.
