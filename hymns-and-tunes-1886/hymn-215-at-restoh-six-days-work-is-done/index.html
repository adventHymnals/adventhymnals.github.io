Hymns and Tunes 1886 215

At rest—oh, six days' work is done

Words: Lowell MasonTune: Hebron. L. M.L.M.

Studio

1At rest—oh, six days' work is done,
Another Sabbath is begun;
Return, my soul, enjoy thy rest,
Improve the day that God has blessed.

2Come, bless the Lord, whose love assigns
So sweet a rest to weary minds;
A blessed anticost below
On this day more than all the seven.

3O that our thoughts and thanks may rise
As grateful incense to his skies,
And draw from Christ that sweet repose
Which none but he that feels it knows.

4This heavenly calm within the breast
Is the best pledge of glorious rest,
Which for the church of God remains,
The end of cares, the end of pains.
