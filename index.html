The Studio

Learn your part before Sabbath.

The score is drawn live from the music, so every hymn in the Studio can be heard any way you need it.

  • Your voice alone. Soprano, alto, tenor or bass, solo or muted so you sing it.
  • At your pace. Slow it down, loop the hard bar, count yourself in.
  • In your key. Transpose, lift the last verse, see the chords.
  • In tune. Sing into the microphone and watch your pitch against the note.
  • On the wall. Project the words for the congregation, verse by verse.
