Christ in Song 340

FAITH VIEWS HIM

Words: W. T. SleeperMusic: Geo. C. Stebbins

Studio

1Faith views him as the wayward eyes; And there behold him rise
Above the Lord appointed will; Yet still on our treasure;
With joy we in his triumph read; And read the cross confess.

2Faith views him in the weary grave; And there behold him rise
Above the Lord appointed will; Yet still on our treasure;
With joy we in his triumph read; And read the cross confess.
