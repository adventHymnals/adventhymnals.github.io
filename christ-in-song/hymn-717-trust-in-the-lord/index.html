Christ in Song 717

Trust in the Lord

Words: J. H. SammisMusic: D. B. Towner

Studio

1Trust in the Lord with all thine heart, And lean not to thine own;
In all thy ways ac-knowl-edge Him, And He shall guide thee on.

RefrainTrust in the Lord, trust in the Lord, And lean not to thine own;
Trust in the Lord, trust in the Lord, And He shall guide thee on.

2Trust in the Lord, though dark thy way, And though thy heart may bleed;
He knows the path that leads to day, He knows thy ev-ery need.

3Trust in the Lord, His love is sure, His prom-is-es are true;
His mer-cies ev-er-more en-dure, What more can He do?
