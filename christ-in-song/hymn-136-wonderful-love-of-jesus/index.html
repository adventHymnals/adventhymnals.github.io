Christ in Song 136

Wonderful Love of Jesus

Studio

1In joy - ful high and ho - ly lays, My soul her grateful voice would raise;
But when can song, the weakness might, In the won - der - ful love of light;
In pain I sing and praise today, The wonderful love of Jesus.

RefrainWon - der - ful love! won - der - ful love!
Won - der - ful love! won - der - ful love!
Won - der - ful love of Jesus!

2A joy - by day - a peace by night, My Saviour makes my darkness bright;
With love my heart is fully blessed, And I can find in Jesus rest.

3I joy - ful feel and ho - ly keep, My soul can feel his presence deep;
In joy - ful - or - der - ful love, Won - der - ful love of Jesus.

Words
E. H. Miner
