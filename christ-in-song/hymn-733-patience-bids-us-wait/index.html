Christ in Song 733

Patience, Bids Us Wait

Studio

1Pa-tience, bids us wait, Till the prom-ise of the Lord is ful-filled;
Trust and not be a-fraid, For the word of the Lord shall be ful-filled.
Thou hast fought the good fight, Hold fast that thou hast till He come.

2Wait on the Lord, be of good cour-age, He will strength-en thy heart;
Wait, I say, on the Lord, And He will give thee thy heart's de-sire.
Thou hast fought the good fight, Hold fast that thou hast till He come.

3They that wait up-on the Lord shall re-new their strength; they shall mount up with wings as ea-gles;
They shall run and not be wea-ry, and they shall walk and not faint.
Thou hast fought the good fight, Hold fast that thou hast till He come.
