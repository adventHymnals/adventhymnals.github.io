Christ in Song 689

We Shall Meet By and By

Words: J. SweneyMusic: Hubert P. Main

Studio

1We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
There our hearts shall rest forever, By and by.

RefrainBy and by, by and by, We shall meet on that beautiful shore;
By and by, by and by, We shall meet on that beautiful shore.

2We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
With our loved ones gone before us, By and by.

3We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
And the storms of life are over, By and by.

4We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
We shall meet beyond the river, By and by, by and by;
Where the light of love is streaming, By and by.

Words
J. Sweney
Music
