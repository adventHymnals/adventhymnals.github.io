SDA Hymnal 24

Every Star Shall Sing a Carol

Words: Sydney Bertram CarterMusic: Sydney Bertram CarterTune: Every Star7.6.7.6.D

Studio

1Every star shall sing a carol,
Every creature, high or low,
Come and praise the King of heaven
By whatever name you know.

RefrainGod above, Man below,
Holy is the name I know.

2When the King of all creation
Had a cradle on the earth,
Holy was the human body,
By whatever name you know.

3Every star and every planet,
Every creature, high or low,
Come and praise the King of heaven
By whatever name you know.
