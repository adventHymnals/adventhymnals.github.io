SDA Hymnal 450

Beautiful Zion

Words: TraditionalMusic: TraditionalTune: BEAUTIFUL ZION7.6.7.6

Studio

1Beautiful Zion, built above,
Beautiful city that I love;

RefrainBeautiful gates of pearly white,
Beautiful rivers clear and bright,
Beautiful robes the ransomed wear,
Beautiful temple, God its light,
Beautiful fountains never dry,
Beautiful all who enter there.

2Beautiful trees forever there,
Beautiful fruit they always bear;

3Beautiful crowns on every brow,
Beautiful palms the conquerors show;
