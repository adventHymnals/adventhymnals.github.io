SDA Hymnal 7

The Lord in Zion Reigneth

Words: Josiah ConderMusic: John HattonTune: Duke StreetL.M.

Studio

1The Lord in Zion reigneth,
Let all the world rejoice,
And come before His throne of grace
With tuneful heart and voice;
The Lord in Zion reigneth,
And there His praise shall ring,
Till distant isles beyond the wave
Exalt the King of kings.

2The Lord in Zion reigneth,
And who so great as He?
The depths of earth are in His hands;
He rules the mighty sea.
O crown His name with honor,
And let His standard wave,
Till every creature owns His power
To conquer and to save.

3The Lord in Zion reigneth,
These hours to Him belong;
Enter now His temple gates,
And fill His courts with song;
O Lord in Zion reigneth,
Be-hold, the open door!
Till He shall come, whose right it is
To rule forevermore.
