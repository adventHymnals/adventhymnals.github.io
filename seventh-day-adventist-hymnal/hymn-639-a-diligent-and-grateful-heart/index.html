SDA Hymnal 639

A Diligent and Grateful Heart

Words: Anonymous

Studio

1A diligent and grateful heart
Prompts me to sing Thy praise.

2I thank Thee for the means to serve
With talents and with tithes,
Thy love and mercies bring
For sharing brings the utmost joy,
Thy people in their needs.

3My thanks I give for stewardship
To minister through deeds,
To serve and share to magnify
From the start
Have blessed me all my days.

4O Lord, I dedicate my care
When lifting others
Thy people in their needs
In deep humility.

Words
Anonymous
Scripture
Malachi 3:10; 2 Corinthians 9:7
