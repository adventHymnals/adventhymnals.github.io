SDA Hymnal 72

Creator of the Stars of Night

Words: Latin hymn, 9th centuryMusic: Plainsong, Mode IVTune: Creator Alme SiderumL.M.

Studio

1Creator of the stars of night,
Thy people's everlasting light,
O Christ, Thou Savior of us all,
We pray Thee, hear us when we call.

RefrainAmen.

2At the great name of Jesus, now
All knees must bend, all hearts must bow;
And things celestial Thee shall own,
And things terrestrial, Lord alone.

3To God the Father, God the Son,
And God the Spirit, Three in one,
Laud, honor, might, and glory be
From age to age eternally.
