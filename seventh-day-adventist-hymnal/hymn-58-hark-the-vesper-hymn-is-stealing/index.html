SDA Hymnal 58

Hark, the Vesper Hymn Is Stealing

Studio

1Hark! the vesper hymn is stealing
O'er the waters soft and clear;
Nearer yet, and nearer pealing,
Soft it breaks upon the ear.

RefrainJubilate! Jubilate!
Jubilate! Amen.

2Now, like moonbeams on the water,
Nearer it draws near;
Jubilate! Jubilate!
Jubilate! Amen.
