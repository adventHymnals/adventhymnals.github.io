Hymns and Tunes 1876 26

Hymn 26

Studio

1HOW holy God's commands !
How just his precepts are !
Come, cast your burdens on the Lord,
And trust his constant care.

2Beneath his watchful eye
His saints securely dwell ;
That hand which bears all nature up,
Shall guard his children well.

3Why should this anxious load
Press down your weary mind ?
Haste to your Heavenly Father's throne,
And sweet refreshment find.

4His goodness stands approved
Through each succeeding day ;
I'll drop my burden at his feet,
And bear a song away.

Dennis (Church Hymnal #64); Federal Street (Christ in Song #335)
