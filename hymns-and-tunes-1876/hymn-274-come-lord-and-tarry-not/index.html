Hymns and Tunes 1876 274

Come, Lord, and tarry not

Studio

1Come, Lord, and tarry not; Bring the long-looked-for day; Oh! why these years of waiting here, These ages of delay?

2Come, for creation groans, Impatient of thy stay; Worn out with these long years of ill, These ages of delay.

  • Creation groaning
  • Delay
  • Impatience
  • Longing
  • Second coming
